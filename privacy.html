Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Fidexa Network ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our Telegram Mini App (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information from you:

  • Telegram User Data: When you use the Service, we receive your Telegram user ID, username, first name, and last name through the Telegram Mini App platform.
  • Usage Data: We collect information on how you interact with the Service, such as your mining activity, referral counts, and task completion status.
  • Communication Data: If you contact us for support, we may collect the content of your messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service.
  • To track your mining balance, referrals, and rewards.
  • To improve and personalize your experience.
  • To communicate with you, including responding to your support requests.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase, a cloud database platform. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. We may share your data if required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Your Rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the "Delete Account" option in the Profile tab of the Mini App. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our Telegram channel: @fidexa_Network.
