Fidexa Network

Fidexa Network is a digital mining and rewards application. The current version uses $FDX as the internal reward/credit unit. Blockchain and token functionality may be introduced during a later development stage.

Fidexa Reward Policy

$FDX

$FDX is the internal reward and credit unit used by the current Fidexa Network application.

Mining

The base mining rate is 1.0 $FDX/hour. A normal mining session lasts 8 hours.

Mining Boosters

Each successfully completed rewarded advertisement can provide a +0.5 $FDX/hour mining-rate increase. A maximum of 5 Booster ads can be completed within a 24-hour Booster window.

Referral Rate

Each qualifying active referral can provide +0.1 $FDX/hour. The referral mining-rate change is applied from the point the referral becomes qualifying and active.

Official Channel Task

The official-channel task provides a one-time +15 $FDX reward after the user's Telegram membership has been successfully verified.

Reward Verification

Tasks that require external actions, such as joining the official channel, must be verified before their reward is credited.

Future Development

Wallet, blockchain and other advanced functionality may be connected during future development. Future functionality is not a guarantee of launch or availability.

Fidexa Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the general way Fidexa Network may handle information used by the application.

Telegram Information

When the Mini App is opened through Telegram, the application may receive basic Telegram-provided account information such as Telegram user ID, first name, last name and username.

Application Data

The current frontend version may store application state locally on the user's device. This can include mining progress, reward balances, Booster counts and referral-preview information.

Support Messages

Support messages entered into the current frontend preview are stored locally until backend support delivery is implemented. A production backend may process support messages for customer assistance.

Advertising

Rewarded advertisements may be used for eligible reward features. A production advertising provider may process information according to its own privacy policy and applicable requirements.

Channel Verification

For the official-channel task, Telegram membership information may be checked by the Fidexa backend through the Telegram platform to determine whether the user is eligible for the task reward.

Security

Production account balances and rewards should be validated by a secure backend. Sensitive credentials such as Telegram bot tokens should never be placed in the Mini App frontend.

Future Changes

This policy may be updated as Fidexa Network develops additional functionality, services and integrations.

Fidexa Network White Paper

1. Overview

Fidexa Network is designed as a digital participation and rewards platform built around mining sessions, tasks, referrals and community participation.

2. $FDX Reward Unit

In the current development stage, $FDX represents an internal reward and credit unit inside the Fidexa application. It should not be represented as a live blockchain token or publicly tradable asset unless and until such functionality is actually implemented.

3. Mining System

Users can start an 8-hour mining session. The base rate is 1.0 $FDX/hour.

4. Mining Boosters

Users can increase their mining rate by completing eligible rewarded advertisements. Each successfully completed Booster ad adds +0.5 $FDX/hour, with a maximum of 5 Booster activations per 24-hour window.

5. Referral System

Qualifying active referrals can increase a user's mining rate by +0.1 $FDX/hour per active referral. The increase applies to eligible active mining from the point the referral becomes active.

6. Community Task

Fidexa includes an official-channel task. A user must actually join the official Telegram channel and pass membership verification before the one-time +15 $FDX reward is credited.

7. Rewarded Advertising

Rewarded advertising is intended to connect advertising participation with selected Fidexa rewards. Production rewards should only be issued from verified ad-completion callbacks supplied by the advertising provider.

8. Security Architecture

A production version should keep balances, reward validation, referral validation and task verification on a secure backend. Client-side values should not be treated as authoritative financial or reward records.

9. Wallet Development

Wallet functionality is currently presented as coming soon. Future wallet functionality may include additional account and transfer infrastructure if implemented.

10. Future Roadmap

  • Secure backend account system
  • Production reward verification
  • Real rewarded-ad integration
  • Server-side referral tracking
  • Wallet infrastructure
  • Potential blockchain integration

These roadmap items describe possible future development and are not guarantees that every feature will launch.
